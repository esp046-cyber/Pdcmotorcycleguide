Quick reference: 8-hour PDC (DL Code A & A1)

  • Always do the pre-ride check first. Skipping it earns a demerit.
  • Braking: about 70% front, 30% rear. Squeeze progressively, never grab. Use engine braking and brake earlier on wet roads.
  • Eyes up: look where you want to go, not at the plank, cone or ground.
  • Clutch: find the friction zone, then feather the clutch and drag the rear brake for slow-speed balance.

T-CLOCS pre-ride check

  • T – Tires & wheels
  • C – Controls (levers, throttle, cables)
  • L – Lights & electrics
  • O – Oil & fluids
  • C – Chassis & chain
  • S – Stands

Test track obstacles

  • Narrow plank: steady throttle, feather clutch, head and eyes up.
  • Slalom: lean the bike, keep your body upright, hold steady throttle.
  • Figure-8: turn your head early, no foot down.
  • Emergency stop: stop cleanly inside the lines without skidding.
  • Hill start: hold the rear brake, release smoothly while adding throttle.

Key rules at a glance

  • Helmet: a PS/ICC-marked helmet is required (RA 10054).
  • Passengers: one passenger only. Children are covered by RA 10666 (Children’s Safety on Motorcycles Act).
  • Top box: stay within LTO limits, and the load must not block lights or plates.
  • Saddlebags: no higher than the seat and no wider than 14 inches from the side.
  • Demerits: stalling, foot down on the plank or Figure-8, hitting cones, skipping the pre-check.

Always verify current requirements with your LTO-accredited driving school or the LTO.
